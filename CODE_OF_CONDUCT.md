# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Relay a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form. We expect everyone
to communicate constructively, respect different viewpoints, and keep safety,
privacy, and the development-stage nature of Relay in mind.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy and patience;
- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- accepting good-faith corrections;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- respecting privacy and not publishing sensitive data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, publishing private information,
threats, and deliberately testing Relay against real emergencies or real
people without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the primary maintainer through a private
GitHub channel. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject changes, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
participants and the project.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
